v1.10 is built, verified and released: the Numbers table is one long table again, and the sideways scrollbar is always on show.

**One long table.** The table no longer scrolls inside a box of its own. It runs the full length of the page and the page does the scrolling, as it did before the frame. What the frame gave you stays: the year headings hold at the top of the window as you scroll down through the table, and the line names hold at the left as you scroll across. The headings now ride with the window rather than with a box, which took a different mechanism underneath, since a heading cannot pin itself to the window from inside a sideways scroller: it shifts down by exactly the distance the page has scrolled, and stops at the table's end.

**The sideways bar.** The table's own scrollbar is hidden and a bar of the same look sits at the foot of the window for as long as any part of the table is in view: drag it and the table moves, scroll the table with a trackpad or a finger and the bar follows. When you reach the end of the table it settles under the last row, and it never covers anything but the table. The three raw-field tables under "Every field EODHD reports" get the same bar. On a browser with overlay scrollbars, Firefox on a Mac for one, the bar's thumb shows only while you hover or scroll; Chrome and Safari draw it all the time.

**Verified.** The v1.7 suite was turned to the new behaviour: it checks the table is as tall as its rows with no bars of its own, that the bar sits at the foot of the window as wide as the table, that scrolling the page keeps the headings and the corner at the top of the window while the rows pass underneath, that the line names stay at the left, that the bar and the table drive each other both ways, that the bar settles at the table's end, that the headings stop at the table's end when there is room below, and the same on a phone; the quarters, ratios, CSV and forty-quarter checks pass unchanged. The three other suites pass, the wrangler dry-run passed and the render check is clean. Screenshots attached above.
