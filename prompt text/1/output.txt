v1.0 is built, verified and released. The main branch now carries a plain page listing all 57 chapters of Warren Buffett and the Interpretation of Financial Statements, grouped the way the book groups them, with no styling as you asked.

**The chapter list.** The titles were checked against the [Library of Congress catalogue record](https://catdir.loc.gov/catdir/enhancements/fy0901/2008277390-t.html) of the book's table of contents, the authoritative listing for the 2008 Scribner edition. The publisher, retailer and catalogue pages themselves are blocked by this container's network policy, so the check went through search-engine snippets of that record rather than the page itself. Every title I could match that way agreed with the record, including two whose exact wording is easy to get wrong: chapter 10 ends "in His Search for Long-Term Gold", and chapter 46 reads "Warren Likes to See This on the Balance Sheet". The page shows the Introduction, then chapters 1 to 6, then the four parts of the book: The Income Statement, The Balance Sheet, The Cash Flow Statement, and Valuing the Company with a Durable Competitive Advantage.

**One title to glance at.** Chapter 25 could not be matched by exact phrase either way. It is on the page as "Inventory: What the Company Needs to Buy and What the Company Needs to Sell", which is how a snippet of the record gave it, though it is sometimes quoted as "Total Inventory: What the Company Needs to Buy and Sell". Your copy of the book will settle it, and it is a one-line change if the record has it wrong.
