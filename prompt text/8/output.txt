v1.7 is built, verified and released. The Numbers table now runs newest first, sits in a frame that keeps its headings and scrollbars in view, and every year heading opens into its quarters.

**The frame.** The table lives in its own window no taller than the screen. Scroll down inside it and the year headings stay pinned at the top; scroll across and the line names stay pinned at the left; both scrollbars are drawn all the time rather than appearing on hover, in the paper's own colours. Below the frame the page carries on as before to the raw-fields section.

**Newest first.** The latest year is now the first column at the left, then back through time to the right, so the table opens on the latest figures without any scrolling. A "2025 to date" column stands before the latest full year whenever EODHD has quarters reported since the last year end.

**Quarters.** Every fetch now keeps the last forty quarters, ten years' worth, alongside the yearly figures. Click a year heading and its quarters unfold to its right, newest first, in a lightly tinted column each; click again to fold them away. Plain figures and the within-period ratios, such as gross margin, are the quarter's own. The four ratios that only make sense against a full year's earnings, return on assets, years to clear the long-term debt, return on equity and capital expenditure against earnings, are built on the trailing four quarters and show a dash until four are there. The CSV follows whatever columns are open. Companies you fetched before this version have no quarters stored, so their headings stay plain until you press Fetch again, and the note above the table says so.
