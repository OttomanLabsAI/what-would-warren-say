v1.4 is built, verified and released. The line above the masthead is gone, and a Numbers tab now sits beside Chapters: give it a ticker and your EODHD key and it lays out the company's full yearly history of the figures the book walks through.

**The Numbers tab.** Two links under the masthead switch between Chapters and Numbers. The Numbers page has a ticker box, a box for your EODHD API token, a Remember tick and Fetch. Tickers take EODHD's form, code dot exchange, such as KO.US or VOD.LSE, and a bare US code gets .US added. Press Fetch and the page shows the company's name, exchange, currency and fiscal year end, then a table with one column per fiscal year, oldest to newest, for every year EODHD holds. The rows follow the book: the income statement from revenue to earnings per share, the balance sheet from cash to treasury stock, and the cash flow lines for capital expenditure, buybacks and dividends. Each row carries the chapter where the book discusses it, and under each ratio sits the book's rule of thumb in italics, from gross margin and SG&A against gross profit to the years of net earnings it would take to clear the long-term debt, debt to equity, return on equity and capital expenditure against net earnings. Negative figures show in claret, missing ones as a dash. Below the table, "Every field EODHD reports" opens the raw statements in full.

**Your key and your calls.** The token is typed on the page and kept only in your browser, and only if you tick Remember. It never goes into the notes export or the repository. Each fetch costs ten EODHD calls, so every company you fetch is kept on your device, the eight most recent, and opens again without a call. Fetch again refreshes one, Forget drops it, and Download CSV gives you the table with chapter and unit columns for a spreadsheet.

**How the call travels.** Browsers do not always let a page call another site directly, and EODHD is blocked from this container, so I could not test their stance. The site therefore carries a tiny Worker that relays the request on its own address: the page sends the ticker with your token in a header, the Worker forwards it to EODHD and passes the reply straight back. It forwards only the fundamentals path, keeps nothing and holds no secret, so there is nothing to set up in Cloudflare. Workers Builds deploys it with this push like everything else.

**One caution.** Because EODHD was unreachable from here, the mapping from the book's lines to EODHD's field names comes from their documentation, with second and third names tried where they vary, rather than from a live reply. Every line was checked against a reply built to their shape. If a line comes up blank for a company where EODHD does have the figure, the raw section under the table will still show it, and the ticker is all I need to fix the mapping.

**Verified.** The wrangler dry-run passed with the Worker and its assets binding. The real Worker ran locally under wrangler dev against a stand-in EODHD and passed nine checks: it serves the pages, the fonts and the themed not-found page, refuses a call without a token, turns the header into EODHD's token parameter, passes EODHD's own refusals straight through, forwards only the fundamentals path and refuses anything but a plain fetch. A headless Chromium run drove the page through ten checks against a reply shaped like EODHD's: the masthead line is gone, the Numbers view opens empty, a lowercase bare ticker becomes KO.US, the book's lines and ratios compute to the expected figures across six years, the raw fields list, a kept company shows again after a reload without a call, the token is forgotten unless remembered and refilled when it is, a second company is kept and can be forgotten, the CSV carries every line, and an unknown ticker, a bad token, a used-up daily limit, an empty reply, a dead connection and blank fields each get a plain message. The notes export is unchanged, the Chapters view still works, and at phone width the table scrolls inside its frame while the page does not. I looked at the screenshots, attached above.
