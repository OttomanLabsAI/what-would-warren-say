v1.2 is built, verified and released. The companion now looks like a financial newspaper, and the book is navigated with a section dropdown and a row of chapter tabs. Everything you had written carries over untouched: the saved text and the export format are exactly as before.

**The look.** Salmon paper, dark ink, claret for the small capitals and the marks, teal for links. The masthead and the chapter headlines are set in Playfair Display, the text and your writing in Newsreader, which was designed for reading news on a screen. Your boxes are white on the paper, the way the broadsheets box their tables. The contents page runs the chapters in two newspaper columns with a dotted rule between entries, and the dateline shows today's date. The not-found page wears the same masthead, under the kicker "Correction". The fonts are copies served from the site itself, so the page still loads nothing from anyone else, and a notice with their copyright lines and licence sits beside them.

**The tabs.** The section dropdown under the masthead lists Contents and the five parts of the book: the opening chapters, the income statement, the balance sheet, the cash flow statement and valuation. Choose a part and a row of numbered tabs appears for its chapters, with the Introduction as "Intro" in the first part. The open chapter's tab is filled in ink, and a claret dot marks every chapter you have written on, appearing as you type. The dropdown opens a part at its first chapter and remembers the last one you had open there, and choosing Contents brings the full list back. Deep links still work: opening a chapter's address sets the dropdown and the tabs to match.
