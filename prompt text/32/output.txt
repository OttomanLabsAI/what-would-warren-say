v1.31 is on main. The account system is built on Firebase and waits only for your project's key.

An Account tab makes an account with a name, a surname, a username and a password, all four required, and signs in on any device. The account keeps your watchlists and your chapter notes. When you sign in, what is on the device meets what the account holds, the newer side winning per list and per chapter, and from then on every change goes up as you make it. Every account has the generic My watchlist, which cannot be deleted while signed in. New list and Rename now ask for a description, which you may leave blank, and it shows in italics under the list's name. Adding a company is back in a new form: an Add to line on a company's page with a dropdown of your lists, the generic one first, and an add-by-ticker form under each list that finds the name when the ticker is on file. Company numbers and your EODHD key never leave the device.

Two decisions you should know about. The page talks to Firebase over its web APIs directly, with no SDK, so it still loads no script from elsewhere. And Firebase has no username sign-in of its own, so behind the scenes a username becomes an address at a reserved domain that can never receive mail; nobody sees it and nothing is sent to it.

To switch it on, in the Firebase console: make a project, enable Email/Password under Authentication, create a Firestore database, then deploy the rules from the repository's firebase folder with the command in the README and send me the project's web API key and project id, or paste them into FIREBASE in the page yourself. Until then the Account tab says accounts are not switched on and everything works on the device as before.

The security rules keep every reader to their own documents: a username is claimed once with the claimant's own id, a profile needs its claim, a list needs a name, at most 280 characters of description and at most 500 companies, the generic list cannot be deleted, and only the notes document is writable under the data section.

Verified: the accounts suite runs the page against the Firebase emulators with the repository's rules: sign-up lands the profile, the username claim and the generic list in the store, a company added from its page and a custom list with a description reach the store within a second, rename and delete follow, a typed chapter note goes up, another account's token is refused on every document and every bad shape, sign-out keeps the device's data, a fresh device signs in and gets the lists and the note and stays signed in across reloads, and a taken username, a bad one and a short password are refused in plain words, leaving nothing half-made. The thirteen earlier suites pass too, with the watchlists suite now covering the add forms and the description in the export. The five tabs made the controls row wrap on desktop, so the tabs and the section dropdown are a little narrower and the row is one line again at 1024 pixels and up. The deploy dry run passes, and the rendered home and not-found pages look as before. I looked over the account page, a list with the add form and the phone forms by eye.
