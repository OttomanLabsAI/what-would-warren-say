v1.14 is built, verified and released: a page on your own machine does the pulling.

**Running it.** From the repository folder, `npm run pull-app` starts the page at 127.0.0.1:8787 and opens it in your browser. Paste your EODHD token once and tick remember if you like: it goes into the data folder as a small file, outside the repository, and the box empties itself as soon as it is sent. Pick a market from the first dropdown, NASDAQ, the New York Stock Exchange, London, Shanghai, Shenzhen, the Korea Stock Exchange, KOSDAQ, Xetra or all of them, or pick one of your fourteen sample watchlists from the second, which greys the market out and pulls just those companies. Two options: pull again what is already on disk, and a cap on how many companies this run. Press Start.

**What it shows.** A progress bar with the count pulled, how many came back with figures, with nothing at EODHD, refused by the plan or in error, and the calls spent this run, ten a company plus one a market list. A second block is EODHD's own count: the calls used today against the daily limit before the run and after it, with your plan's name, so you see exactly what the run cost on their side. A third lists the files written and where, and a log runs underneath. Stop ends a run after the fetches already in flight and says how many companies are left; the next Start carries on from there, as does a run that hits the day's limit.

**The files.** The same data folder as the command line puller, which now shares one engine with the page: the universe, the manifest, each raw reply gzipped, each company in the shape the Numbers page keeps, a row of the book's ratios per company, and for each market a screen file as JSON for the site and now also as CSV for a spreadsheet. A watchlist pull files its companies by their exchange suffix.

**Verified.** A browser suite drives the page against a stand-in EODHD: the nine markets and fourteen watchlists in the menus, a start without a token, a NASDAQ pull with the token remembered, EODHD's count before and after, the files on disk and listed on the page, a watchlist pull on the remembered token, and a stop in the middle of a slow run followed by a start that carries on. The command line's harness still passes on the shared engine. The site is unchanged; the wrangler dry-run passed and the render check is clean. Screenshot attached above.
