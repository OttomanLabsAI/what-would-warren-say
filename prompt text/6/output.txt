v1.5 is built, verified and released. Switching between Chapters and Numbers no longer moves the page.

**What moved, and why.** Two things, both in my stylesheet. The contents list is tall enough to need a scrollbar and the empty Numbers view is not, so when the bar came and went the centred column shifted sideways on wide windows and narrowed on narrower ones. Separately, at around a thousand pixels wide the row of controls wrapped onto two lines in Chapters, because the section dropdown insisted on the width of its longest entry, while the same row stayed on one line in Numbers, so everything below jumped by the height of a line.

**What changed.** The page now reserves the scrollbar's space whether or not a bar is showing, so the column keeps its width and position in every view. The dropdown starts from a fixed, modest width and grows into the free space instead of demanding its full natural width, which keeps the controls on one row at desktop widths. And on Numbers the dropdown's slot is kept, invisibly, so the row has exactly the same height in both views. On a phone the dropdown row still collapses on Numbers, because keeping an empty band there looked worse than the small jump, so that is the one place the two views still differ.

**Verified.** Measured in a browser with real, space-taking scrollbars, switching between the contents, a chapter and Numbers moves the masthead title, the controls row, the buttons and the top of the text by zero pixels at six desktop widths from 1440 down to 820, and the controls stay on one row at all of them. Both browser suites still pass, nineteen checks across the Chapters and Numbers views, the wrangler dry-run passed and the render check is clean.
