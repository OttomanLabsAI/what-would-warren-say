What the site does not hold is the rest of your pull: the full history of the other seventeen and a half thousand companies sits in the data folder on your machine, which is EODHD-licensed and never goes into the repository. It reaches the site only through the store, the R2 bucket, which does not exist yet. That is the one thing missing. To fill it:

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, open R2 Object Storage and create a bucket named wwws-data.
2. Under Manage R2 API Tokens, make a token with Object Read & Write on that bucket, and note its access key, its secret and the account's S3 endpoint.
3. On your machine, run `npm run pull-app`, open the second form, "Send to the site", paste the endpoint and the keys, and send. Every company with statements goes up, with the screen files and the index; the raw replies never do.
4. Tell me the bucket exists. I uncomment the binding line in wrangler.jsonc and release, and from then on every company in your pull opens with its full history and no key, the Screen reads from the store, and the home page says what the store holds.

A key stays needed for two things only: the share price and its chart, which are never stored, and fetching from EODHD a fresh copy, or a company that is not in the pull.
