v1.11 is built, verified and released: the puller exists, and the site itself is unchanged.

**What it does.** `tools/pull-fundamentals.mjs` runs on your Mac with your token in an environment variable, never in the repository or the site. By default it takes the United States, keeping NASDAQ and NYSE from EODHD's US list, then London, Shanghai and Shenzhen, around thirteen thousand companies in all; the exchanges and the US venues are options, so Hong Kong is one word away if you want it. Each company costs ten calls, so the four exchanges take two days of your allowance: the run stops by itself when EODHD says the day's calls are spent, keeps everything it has, says how many companies are left, and carries on from there next time. Four fetches run at once, retries cover EODHD's rate limit and dropped connections, a company EODHD has nothing for is written down as missing, and one the plan refuses as refused.

**What lands in data/.** The folder is ignored by git and must stay out of the repository: it is EODHD's licensed data. In it: the universe of symbols with names and venues, a manifest of what was pulled and when, each raw reply byte for byte and gzipped so the mapping can be improved without buying the pull again, each company in exactly the shape the Numbers page keeps, one row per company with the book's ratios for the latest year and ten years of earnings per share, and a screen file per venue, NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE, SHG and SHE, with an index of their counts and dates. A refresh is one option, and monthly is plenty; `--screen-only` rebuilds the screens offline.

**To run it.** `EODHD_TOKEN=your-token npm run pull` from the repository folder, with Node 18 or later. Start with `--limit 20` to watch a few come through, then let it go. Expect a few gigabytes on disk for the four exchanges.

**What comes next, and the decisions still open.** Next is the bucket the Worker reads from, then the Numbers page reading the pull and a screen view. Before those: the whole site behind Cloudflare Access, or only the data routes; the screen's tests, the book's rules or your own; and whether to add Hong Kong.

**Verified.** A mock EODHD drove the tool through a full pull, the shape on disk (raw reply byte for byte, forty quarters, the ratios checked against the figures), resuming, refreshing, named symbols, running out of calls and carrying on, offline rebuilding, and a bad or missing token. I cannot reach EODHD from here, so the first real run is yours; if the symbol lists surprise it, say so. The wrangler dry-run passed and the render check is clean.
