v1.35 is live on main, with the three things you asked for.

The company box now takes a name as well as a ticker and suggests companies as you type, up to eight, from everything on file: the copies kept on the device first, then the sample companies, then every name the pull knows, with each one's ticker, venue and where it comes from. A ticker or its code comes first, then a name that starts with what you typed, then a word that does, then anything that contains it, with accents, punctuation and a leading "The" ignored, so "loreal" finds L'Oréal and "coca" finds The Coca-Cola Company ahead of London's 0QZK listing of the same shares. The arrows move the highlight, Enter opens the highlighted company, a click or a tap opens one, and opening is from file, with no call spent. Typing a ticker and pressing Fetch works as before; Enter on a name opens its best match; a short word that only turns up inside names is taken for a ticker and fetched. The Screen's own find box already matched names.

Every account now carries a secret key, made at sign-up from 24 random bytes and shown on the Account page with Copy and Make a new key. It says a request is yours, in an email for instance. A new key replaces the old one at once, after a confirm, and the old one stops working; an account made before this gets its key the next time it signs in. The key lives in your profile, which the rules let nobody else read, and it never travels in an export. For the email side to come, a server with the project's admin credentials can look a key up in the profiles to find whose it is.

Your new pull is on the site: the Korea Stock Exchange, KOSDAQ, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Xetra now sit beside London, NASDAQ and New York, 18,218 companies with 17,609 of them carrying their latest year, each opening from its row and screening by venue, and the search knows them all. The names file for the search is built with the screen files from now on and goes to the store with them; the manifest and the universe you sent are the pull's own records and the site needs nothing from them.

Verified: a new suite drives the search, the suggestions and their order, the arrows, Enter, a click, a tap, the accents, the fallback without the names file and the phone layout; the accounts suite on the Firebase emulators checks the key at sign-up, its replacement, its arrival on a fresh device, an older account getting one, and the rules refusing a key of the wrong shape; the pull engine and the pull page were driven against the mocks with the names file in the screens and in the send; every other browser suite, the dry run and the render check pass, and the search list and the Account page were inspected on desktop and phone.
