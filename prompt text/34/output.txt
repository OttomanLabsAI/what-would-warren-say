v1.33 is live on main. Every company now carries its stock splits from EODHD, option 1 as you chose: the pull fetches each company's whole split history right after its fundamentals, one call a company, and keeps it with the company, and a live fetch on the site does the same through the relay. The share-price chart divides every bar before a split by the splits since its date, so a four-for-one no longer reads as a crash, and the line under the chart says which splits it is restated for. The company page lists the splits under the next report date, newest first. A company pulled or kept before this asks for its list once when its chart is drawn, and keeps it.

One finding changed the shape of the work. Your own pull shows EODHD already restates its per-share figures for later splits: Apple's 2019 shares read about 18.6 billion and its earnings per share for the quarter to September 2019 read 0.76, both the four-for-one figures, and Nvidia, Tesla, Amazon, Alphabet and Broadcom read the same way, down to the balance-sheet share count. So the per-share lines in the table and the Screen are left as they come, and the split list serves the chart and the page.

For the companies already on disk, the pull page has a Fetch splits button, and the command line a --splits option, which ask for the split history of every company without one, one call each, about 9,300 calls for what is on file, stopping on the day's limit like a pull; a rebuild from the saved replies carries the lists too. A fetch now costs eleven calls rather than ten.

Verified: the price chart suite checks the split list fetched once a company and the chart restated, by 4 over ten years and by 28 over all time for Apple's two splits, with the figures and the hover reading the restated prices, and a failed list leaving the chart as traded with a note. The fetch suite checks the list fetched after the fundamentals and kept with the company, and the company kept when that call fails. The real Worker under wrangler dev forwards the splits path and refuses the others.
